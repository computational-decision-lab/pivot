## 2026-08-25 — Practical-effect thresholds frozen before rerun

The initial pilot thresholds (0.05 CTI, 0.05 IDE, and 0.02 strategic effect)
were below a practically meaningful unit for the configured horizons and made
the sub-Gaussian sample rule infeasible. Before inspecting the expanded
confirmatory outputs, the registry was frozen at 5 CTI units over 30 rounds,
0.5 held-out transition IDE, and 0.5 paired strategic return units. These are
measurement-scale thresholds, not directional tuning; the old pilot remains
archived and is not reclassified.

The 256-paired-unit E3b confirmatory attempt failed the preregistered
non-perfect-proxy gate (observer/actor correlation 0.00794). The discrete
observer hit-rate was therefore unable to measure transfer at scale. The run
is frozen under `results/v7/e3b-confirmatory-invalid-proxy`; no hypothesis
direction was inspected for promotion. The development-only redesign replaces
that score with a short-horizon direct replay of the public environment reward
using fixed replay opponents. The actor retains the full MPE2 rollout and
adaptive feedback, while MPE2 dynamics, T=30, K=8, the HF budget, and the CTI
estimand remain unchanged. A new confirmatory classification is required
