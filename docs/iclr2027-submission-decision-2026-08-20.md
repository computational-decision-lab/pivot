# ICLR 2027 Submission Decision

Date: 2026-08-20 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Decision: CONDITIONAL GO

The local PIVOT package is ready for final author review under the official
ICLR 2027 anonymous format. The official call and author guidelines specify a
9-page main-text limit, double-blind review, supplementary uploads, and a
mandatory AI use statement. The package implements those requirements and
records the retrieved rules in
`/opt/projects/research/pivot/paper/iclr2027/official_rules.json`.

## What is complete

- Official ICLR 2027 style assets are tracked with archive and file hashes.
- The anonymous PDF is rebuilt from
  `/opt/projects/research/pivot/paper/iclr2027/main.tex`.
- The PDF has 9 main-text pages and 10 pages total including references and
  appendix; citations and fonts pass the local verifier.
- The deterministic supplementary archive contains the code, configs, tests,
  frozen snapshot, and generated tables without raw vendor archives or private
  paths.
- The delivery auditor writes
  `/opt/projects/research/pivot/paper/iclr2027/submission_verification.json`.

## What prevents an unconditional GO

OpenReview profile, author quota, reciprocal-review, conflict, author-metadata,
and dual-submission checks require the actual authors. Scientifically, the
controlled reversal and strategic fixture are supported, but causal interactive
market response, external strategic validation, and a confirmatory external
update rule remain open. The Binance audit is explicitly observational and has
no observed reversal; it is not a causal promotion gate.

The package therefore supports final author review and a later submission
decision. It does not claim that a paper has been uploaded or accepted.
