# Rights and third-party dependencies

This release preserves existing third-party notices and does not introduce a
blanket license over the authors' and collaborator's code. The repository had
no project-wide license at the start of this release. Availability for review
is not a grant of unrestricted redistribution or relicensing rights.

Dependencies are installed from their upstream distributions and retain their
own licenses. In particular, OpenSpiel, MeltingPot and its native dependencies,
HighwayEnv, MetaDrive, PettingZoo/MPE2, NumPy, Matplotlib, and PyMuPDF are not
relicensed by this package. Simulator assets and pretrained models are not
silently bundled; any required installation or download is documented with
the corresponding experiment environment.

The source archive preserves code headers and supplied notices. Operational
credentials and private connection material are excluded. The source manifests
record which research files are included and why non-research operational
files are omitted.

The ICLR manuscript style files retain their supplied notices. Figure 4 is a
supplied final artifact and is identified as such in the figure map.
